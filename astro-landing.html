How It Works

Plan, Execute, Decide

The workflow turn-by-turn tools can't provide: structured planning, parallel execution, strategic decisions from anywhere.

Plan the Work

Decompose complex goals into coordinated tasks with explicit dependencies. Turn your moonshot into structured missions—not a flat todo list, but a graph showing what can run in parallel and what must wait. Your plan guides all downstream execution.

Execute in Parallel

Launch multiple independent tasks simultaneously across environments. While one trains on HPC, another builds UI locally, a third deploys infrastructure—all from one plan. Turn-by-turn tools can only do one thing at a time. Astro orchestrates many.

Decide Anywhere

Make strategic decisions from your phone, laptop, anywhere. Approach A worked but B is stuck—pivot or debug? This branch looks promising—double down or explore alternatives? You're not tied to a terminal. Review progress and make calls on your schedule.

Our Mission

Astro is mission control for ambitious builders and scientists. We provide the planning and orchestration layer for long-horizon work—helping you structure complex goals, coordinate execution across environments, and surface the critical moments that require human judgment. When should you pivot? Which branches deserve continued investment? Where does your unique strategic insight matter most? Astro amplifies human decision-making at the scale of complexity that once required entire teams.
