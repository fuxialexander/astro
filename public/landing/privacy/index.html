Privacy Policy

How Astro collects, uses, and protects your data. We believe in transparency and giving you control over your information.

Effective: March 12, 2026
01

Overview

Astro ("we", "us", "our") is a planning and coordination platform for builders and scientists. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use our platform, including the web application, CLI tools, and agent runner.

By using Astro, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

02

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

  • Authentication credentials (passkeys, WebAuthn tokens)
  • Display name and profile information you choose to provide
  • Team membership and role information

Usage Data

  • Plans, tasks, and project structures you create
  • Execution logs and streaming events from task dispatch
  • AI token usage metrics (cost, model, turns per session)
  • Device registration information for connected machines

Technical Data

  • Browser type, operating system, and device information
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Error logs and performance metrics
03

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide the service: Plan generation, task dispatch, execution coordination, and real-time collaboration
  • Improve the platform: Analyze usage patterns to improve plan quality and dispatch efficiency
  • Maintain security: Detect abuse, prevent unauthorized access, and protect our infrastructure
  • Communicate: Send service-related notifications, such as approval requests and execution results
  • Legal compliance: Meet applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests
04

AI and Your Data

Astro uses AI services (currently Anthropic's Claude) to generate plans, decompose tasks, and assist with strategic decisions. When you use AI-powered features:

  • Your project context (vision document, task descriptions, dependencies) is sent to the AI provider to generate relevant outputs
  • AI interactions are logged as streaming events for execution recovery and session continuity
  • We do not use your project data to train AI models
  • AI provider data handling is subject to the provider's own privacy policy

Your code stays on your machines. Astro dispatches tasks to your registered devices (laptop, HPC, cloud). Your source code and execution environments remain under your control. The platform coordinates — it does not host your code.

05

Local Mode

Astro offers a fully local mode that runs entirely on your machine using PGlite (Postgres-in-WASM). In local mode:

  • No data is transmitted to external servers
  • No authentication is required
  • All data is stored locally in your browser and on your machine
  • AI features still require an API key and will communicate with the AI provider
06

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Team members: Project data is shared with members of your team, subject to role-based access controls
  • AI providers: Task context is sent to AI services for plan generation and chat features
  • Infrastructure providers: We use hosting providers (Fly.io, Cloudflare, Supabase) to operate the service
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process
07

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Streaming events and execution logs are retained for session recovery and debugging purposes.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us. Some data may be retained as required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., fraud prevention).

08

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • WebAuthn/passkey authentication (no passwords stored)
  • TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Row-level security (RLS) in the database ensuring users only access their own data
  • JWT-based session management with secure token handling
  • Isolated execution environments — your code runs on your machines, not ours
09

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data for certain purposes
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address listed below.

10

Children's Privacy

Astro is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a revised effective date. Your continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
